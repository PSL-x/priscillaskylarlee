Vancouver has a pretty rich coffee scene, with a blend of "ol'faithful" sorts of cafes, and micro-roasteries. Specifically for pourover, we lost a few spots in the COVID era, but here's my top picks of 2026.

## Top 5 Pourover Spots

1. Peace and Culture Coffee
2. Yuan Coffee
3. Hooray Coffee
4. Prototype Coffee
5. Noct Coffee

## Ol'Faithful Spots

**Breka**  
24/7, reasonably priced, and delicious bread and pastries. Honestly their coffee is meh, but makes the list because every food item there is delightful, and they have the best Bienenstich cake I've had in Vancouver.

**Nemesis**  
Definitely grew a ton in the last 5 years, but consistently solid drinks and pastries, and the base coffees are phenomenal. Try 'The Juice' if you haven't – my favourite basic blend.

**CafeClub**  
Online order ahead model, featuring coffee from Nemesis. Meant to be a quick stop for coffee and treats from local bakeries, but they've started to expand their menu to have sandwiches and other fountain drinks (I love their lemon ginger drink). Bonus for the cute character logo.

## Bonus: Favourite Roasters
**The Barn | Berlin, Germany**  
Every time I stumble across a bag from The Barn, I love it. Was able to finally go to the roastery in Berlin, and everything I had was... amazing, as expected. 

**Traffic | Montreal, Canada**  
These folks make some funky coffee, and boy is it good. Probably most known for their "no-name" coffee, they definitely hit their tasting notes – speaking of which, who wouldn't want their coffee to taste like 'outer space'? 

**Raw Sugar Roast | Tokyo, Japan**  
When I smelt my first bag from them I felt like I got punched in the face. It had such vibrant flavours. 

### To Harken Coffee, RIP my favourite ever

Shoutout to my forever favourite Harken Coffee – I miss your pourover and Cereal deeply.
