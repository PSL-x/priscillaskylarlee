Leading standardization efforts across SAP's Data & Analytics product suite, I serve as the voice for our line of business in cross-organizational alignment discussions with representatives from all SAP product lines.


## Role
I run internal audits to identify gaps, build cases for our product needs, and work with PM and Development to get standards adoption on roadmaps. Much of the work involves advocating when proposed standards don't fit our existing use cases. Working with my product area leads, I liaise with representatives from other products to share concerns, gathering feedback from their use cases and building collective cases for why certain standards need adjustment or exceptions. These conversations help grow understanding of needs across product lines, widening the lens of the SAP Design System.

## Outcomes & Impact
Across our product suite, we've moved from 30% compliance with the SAP User Experience Consistency product standards to 70% with the rest planned and on track. The effort continues as an ongoing process of navigating political dynamics, advocating for our products, and ensuring consistent alignment across the portfolio. In addition, this effort has strengthened our product's relationship with other teams by aligning and having open conversations about our design system.

## Notes

- The hardest part isn't defining or implementing standards — it's navigating the gap between what's "compliant" and what customers already expect
- Our products are rooted in custom code and non-standard behaviors that have become user mental models
- Standardization means constantly finding the median between company-wide alignment and product-specific reality
- Standing up for the product while pushing for company-wide consistency is slow, political work (worth it).
