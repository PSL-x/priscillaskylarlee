Leading a small team, I drive the creation of a centralized component library for SAP's fragmented Data & Analytics suite, where multiple teams were independently rebuilding similar elements and accumulating design debt.

![DNA Design System component library](/images/work/dna-component-details.png)

## Role

I am responsible for aligning design, engineering, and product stakeholders across product lines to establish governance, define priorities, and guide the strategic direction. So far, the team has built 15+ accessible custom elements with full Figma functionality, usage guidelines, and developer specifications. We have developed processes / defined roles for working within our team, how we work with our product teams, and how we work with the larger SAP design system team.

## Outcomes & Impact
The design system has reduced design-to-development time by 30%, achieved WCAG 2.1 AA compliance across all elements, and has been adopted by all DNA product teams. We are constantly evolving our internal processes to ensure we have a feedback cycle within our product teams, prioritizing cross-consistency components to be tackled as we continuing growing the Design System.

## Notes
Some things that are top of mind:
- As AI tools accelerate interface production, design systems must evolve beyond component libraries into ethical frameworks
- Define what deserves consistency, what needs guardrails, and what requires human judgment
- Documentation becomes an ethical compass, not just a reference
- Developing Skills / MCP Server to host for the teams to utilize and consume in designers AI use cases, connecting our elements directly to any AI tool for ease of use
